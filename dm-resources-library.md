# 1. Cold Lead SOP

> **Purpose:** Triage every new inbound (story reply, post engager, ad commenter, new follower, keyword DM) and decide HOT path vs. COLD path within 2 messages.

## ENTRY POINTS (where the lead comes from)

- New follower
- Story reply on IFCA or Taeler IG
- Poll voter
- Hand-raise / DM keyword for a free training
- Engaged with an ad or post (pulled from Meta Business Manager)
- Re-spark from an old convo

## OPENER (every cold lead — warm + human)

> "Hey [Name]! 👋 I really appreciate you checking out our content. Just curious — are you here mostly for free trainings and podcasts, or are you working on growing your online fitness business right now?"

## GAUGE TEMPERATURE

**🚀 HOT** — They say something like:
- "I want to start my coaching business"
- "I want more clients"
- "How do I scale my online coaching?"
- "I'm a PT trying to go online"

**🧊 COLD** — They say something like:
- "Just browsing your content"
- "Love the podcasts"
- "Not coaching yet, just learning"
- Generic / vague answer

## HOT SCRIPT & PATH

> "Awesome — I'd love to ask you a few quick questions to see how our team might be able to help you out. Are you against me asking a few questions? No worries either way!"

### Next steps if HOT:
- **If they say YES + a setter/closer is on shift:** Hand off immediately in Slack `#rcs_handoff` or via GHL with quick context.
- **If they're responsive & engaged but no closer available:** Tag in AirTable as HOT, schedule handoff for next available block.
- **If their replies are short/unengaged once you dig in:** Shift them back to COLD path → run Nurture SOP.

## COLD SCRIPT & PATH

> "Awesome — we've got over 600 podcast episodes and free trainings to help you out for free. Out of curiosity, what topics would be most helpful for you right now? Marketing, content, sales — or something else?"

### When they answer:
Drop a matching podcast or training link from the **Podcast Library** (Section 6 below).

> "Perfect! Here's one of our most popular episodes on that: [LINK]. Give it a listen and come back to let me know what you think!"

### Set the follow-up expectation:

> "Cool — I'll check back here in about 24 hours to see what you thought, sound good?"

### 24-hour follow-up:

> "Hey [Name]! 👋 Just circling back — did you get a chance to check out that episode? Curious what you thought!"

### Branching after 24-hour check-in:
- **They reply with takeaways + reveal goals →** flip to HOT script → qualify → handoff
- **Still cold →** move to **Nurture SOP** (Section 2)
- **No reply →** start the **Respark cadence** (Section 3)

> **Purpose:** Move cold or lukewarm leads deeper by sharing high-value training that builds trust and positions IFCA's method. Main goal: get them to watch the **Client Engine Method Video** or **AI Webinar Replay.**

# 3. Resparks SOP

> **Purpose:** Revive conversations that died off + reactivate cold/dormant threads. **60% of booked calls come from resparks**, not fresh openers. This is where the money lives.

## PRIORITY 1: Active Conversations That Died Off

### Follow-Up Timing

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **Day 1** | One follow-up message (shortcut in IG and GHL): *"Checking in here — did you see my last message? I know I can lose things in here 😅"* |
| **Day 2** | Engage with their content/stories on IG (like, comment, view stories). **Must do this from the IG app.** |
| **Day 3** | If still no response → move to **Non-Responders** opportunity in GHL |

### For convos that died off 1+ month ago

**Option A — Soft check-in:**
> "Hey [Name]! I just noticed our conversation died off back in [TIME, e.g., April]. I just wanted to see how [reference something from prior convo] is going."

**Option B — Even softer:**
> "Hey [Name]! Just noticed our convo died off back in [TIME]. [Reference something specific from prior convo]"

**Option C — If a call was pitched but never booked:**
> "So sorry we weren't able to connect and help you further. I am still happy to set you up with a call if you're interested. Did you ever get any help in your business? I ask because we have over 600+ podcast episodes for online fitness coaches growing their biz. Would love to send over something that could help ya!"

**Option D — Casual bump:**
> "Hey [Name], just bumping this up — did you see my last message?"

**Option E — Humor bump (with GIF):**
> "GIF: 'I refuse to believe you'd leave me on read like this 😆'"

**Option F — Empathy + value drop:**
> "Hey [Name], I know people are pitching you nonstop — I'm just here to drop some free value if you're down to check it out."

### Last-Resort Follow-Up: Send the Method Video

If they seem interested but non-committal:

> "Would you be against me sharing a quick 10-min video on how we help coaches grow their business without burning out?"

**If yes → send:** `https://www.impactcoachacademy.com/clients-s`

**If no response in 24 hours → send it anyway:**
> "Hey, you seem busy, no worries — I'm going to drop the video here anyways. I'll check back in soon to see if you have any questions!"

## THE 3 FOLLOW-UP TACTICS (Use These When Resparks Stall)

These are layered on top of the timing cadence above. When the standard bump isn't working, deploy one of these:

### 1️⃣ APPEAL TO THEIR IDENTITY + SAFETY

> Speak to who they want to become AND check on them like a human.

**Word track (adapted for fitness/nutrition coaches):**

> "Hey [Name], hope everything is okay! You submitted an app the other day, and we were working on scheduling a call, and you didn't seem like the type of person to ghost. I want to make sure everything is alright or if I can answer any questions."
>
> SEND
>
> "Let me know if you're okay!"

**Why it works:** Lowers their defenses (you're not pitching, you're checking in), and the "you didn't seem like the type of person to ghost" line subtly anchors them to a higher version of themselves.

### 2️⃣ HIT 'EM WITH A GIF (CHUCK NORRIS, BUT GET CREATIVE)

> Humor lowers sales resistance. If you can get a laugh, you'll get a reply.

**Tools to use:**
- giphy.com
- knowyourmeme.com
- reddit.com/r/memes
- imgflip.com (for custom GIFs with the prospect's name)

**Example:** The classic "My 1st message: 😊 / My 2nd message: 😂 / My 3rd message with no reply: 💥 (Chuck Norris with a gun)" meme.

**Why it works:** Pattern interrupt. They expect another sales pitch. They get a meme. They reply.

### 3️⃣ GET A NO TO GET A YES

> Give them a safe space to say no, and they'll often say yes.

**Word track (adapted for fitness coaches):**

> "Hey [Name], I take it you're not trying to [GOAL or PROBLEM DISCUSSED] anymore?"

**Example A (online fitness coach):**
> "Hey Sarah, I take it you're not trying to fix the lead-flow issue we talked about — where you said you were burning out posting every day with no clients showing up?"

**Example B (PT transitioning online):**
> "Hey Mike, I take it you're not trying to make that switch from in-person sessions to online clients anymore? I know you said the income drop was scary."

**Example C (nutrition coach):**
> "Hey Jordan, I take it you're not trying to hit that 10-client milestone before the end of the quarter anymore?"

**Common response you'll get:**
> "No, I am! I've just been swamped — can we chat tomorrow?"

**Why it works:** Gives them control (subconscious safety) + subtly challenges their character. Both trigger response.

## The Reality Check on Follow-Ups

> A lack of response is usually due to a **lack of belief or confidence in the prospect.** Our certainty must override their doubt.

**The math:**
- Leads have an 11% chance of an active conversation on the first reach-out
- Only 2–3% chance of buying on the first touchpoint
- But after the **6th touchpoint**, there's an **80% chance** of meaningful conversation and **20–30% chance of a sale**

**The rule:** Reach Out → Follow Up → Follow Up Some More → Sign More Clients → Change More Lives → Repeat.

# 7. Mindset & Word Tracks

> Principles that govern HOW you respond, not just what you send. Read these once a week.

## The RAQ Formula (Repeat / Acknowledge / Question)

> **Never make a prospect feel like what they're doing is wrong.** This is the #1 reason DM threads die.

When someone tells you what they're currently doing (their lead-gen approach, their offer pricing, their training method), **do NOT explain why it won't work or why your method is better.** Instead:

1. **Repeat** what they said back to them
2. **Acknowledge** them positively (make them feel seen)
3. **Question** — ask a follow-up that leads them to admit, themselves, that what they're doing isn't working

**Why this works:** When you tell someone they're wrong, you're saying "I'm smarter than you." That's a direct attack on their self-esteem. They retaliate by getting defensive — and the thread dies.

When they admit the struggle themselves, the call is in the bag.

**Example follow-ups:**
> "If you don't mind me asking, how has that been working?"
>
> "What's your biggest frustration with how that's been going?"
>
> "What's been the result with that approach over the last few months?"

> **The principle:** People will do anything for those who **encourage their dreams, justify their failures, allay their fears, confirm their suspicions, and help them throw rocks at their enemies.**
>
> The more you can build rapport and make them feel special, the more likely they are to book.
>
> Be HYPER friendly. Emojis, exclamation points, GIFs (especially GIFs) all humanize you. Just be super, super nice. And always agree.

---

## The "Don't Stress About Single 'No's" Mindset

> The energy spent trying to save one lost deal could've landed you a new client already. **Successful people forget fast.**

If you find yourself ruminating on one "no" from a prospect — stop. That's a signal your pipeline is thin. If your pipeline was stacked, you wouldn't have time to overthink it.

It's not a *them* problem. It's a *you* problem. Thank them for showing you that you have a sourcing issue, and go fill the top of the funnel.

## "Can We Just Skip to the Bottom Line?" / Annoying Prospect Questions

> Common variants: "Can you just tell me the price?" "Does your offer include XYZ?" "What's this gonna cost me?"
>
> The common thread: they don't respect the sales process yet.

### The Word Track

**Prospect:** "Hey — can we just skip to the bottom line here? How much is this gonna cost?"

**You:** "Yep, no worries. *[pause for 2 seconds — this is the most important part]*
>
> I know you wanna get right into the details and go over price, terms, what's included, and all that — and DEFINITELY we're gonna get there. But everything we do here is all customized.
>
> So instead of taking you through the laundry list of every single thing we can do for clients, at this stage what's probably gonna be the most appropriate next step is to ask you a few questions about where you're at… and then based on that, I can make a recommendation and give you the information that's relevant and useful to you specifically.
>
> Does that sound fair?"

### Why It Works

1. **The pause defuses pressure.** It cuts the tension. Don't skip it.
2. **You reposition the call as 100% customized** — not a cookie-cutter solution.

Once you do that, most "annoying" prospects shut up and let you do your job.

## When Someone Asks "How Much Does Your Coaching Cost?" (in DMs)

> Never answer with a number. Ever. Pricing without context kills the conversation.

### Option A — The Soft Answer

> "It depends on your goal."

That's it. Three words. Then ask a follow-up question to get them on a call where price can be discussed with context.

### Option B — The Sharper Answer (for thicker-skinned prospects)

> "Well, are you only shopping based on price? Or value? Because if it's price, I can tell you I'm NOT the cheapest, but I'm the best. I'm usually the last coach someone needs to hire because they learn how to sustain on their own.
>
> Now, there are some cheaper, more cookie-cutter coaches out there who'll gladly advertise their price, because they don't really care if the person gets results.
>
> I only work with people who I know I can help, so I'd first need to get on a call with you and explore what your goals are so I can build a program specific to you."
